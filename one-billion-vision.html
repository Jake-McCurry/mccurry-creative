One Billion Vision

Connecting a printed proposal to the resources behind it.

Organization

JesusOnline Ministries

Project type

Proposal graphics · Print-to-web

My role

Proposal refinement, graphics, QR codes, and companion-page implementation

The challenge

A printed proposal needs a clear visual sequence, yet much of the material a reader may want to explore lives online.

My approach

I refined the proposal’s charts, maps, and visual hierarchy around the ministry’s supplied content. I worked on QR placement and the companion links page, grouping videos, websites, giving information, and accountability resources so a reader could continue from paper to the web.

In-house production and refinement of a ministry proposal. The project title refers to the ministry’s vision.
