JO Equip

A ministry library organized around what leaders come to teach.

Visit the live website ↗

Organization

JesusOnline Ministries

Project type

Resource library · UX

My role

Platform implementation, content organization, and navigation

The challenge

A pastor might arrive looking for a five-minute teaching resource or an entire book. A single list of article titles makes both tasks harder.

My approach

I work with the ministry team to organize resources by audience, topic, and format. Leader Kits keep related teaching materials together. Devotional series have a reading order, and books, playlists, and topical resources have distinct routes through the library.

Ongoing in-house platform work. Ministry authors and contributors supply the teaching content.
