Biking Bigfoot

Illustration · Logo design

A Northwest character on two wheels, drawn as a one-color mark.

Bigfoot rides upright between two evergreens. The one-color drawing keeps the rider’s outline strong and leaves room for the finer lines of the frame and spokes.

The print mockup uses the original illustration. The black-and-white artwork below shows the full design at a larger scale.
