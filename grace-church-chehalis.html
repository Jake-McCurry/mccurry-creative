Grace Church Chehalis

Keeping local events, youth ministry, and the sermon archive current.

Organization

Grace Church Chehalis

Project type

WordPress · Editorial publishing

My role

Website updates, event presentation, and sermon publishing

The challenge

A family checking the next youth event and a listener catching up on Sunday’s sermon need different information. Both depend on dates and links staying current.

My approach

I update event sections, organize student-ministry material supplied by church staff, and publish sermons with their service date, speaker, series, and video. Consistent archive entries let listeners return to a particular message or follow a series.

Ongoing website support. Sermon and student-ministry content belong to the church and its speakers.
