JesusOnline billboard landing pages

Taking Purpose for Life and Gift of Heaven from a short message to a useful next step.

Organization

JesusOnline Ministries

Project type

Mobile outreach pages

My role

Landing-page layout, media placement, and response routing

The challenge

Someone opening a page after seeing a billboard has very little context. The page needs to acknowledge what brought them there and make the next action easy to find on a phone.

My approach

My work covers Purpose for Life and Gift of Heaven. I worked on the opening message and mobile spacing, arranged the ministry’s content, and connected response choices to follow-up resources. Gift of Heaven is a public example of the response experience.

In-house landing-page work using ministry content. The linked page illustrates the experience; individual billboard destinations are subject to campaign confirmation.
